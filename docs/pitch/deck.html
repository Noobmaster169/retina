The product · 3 of 7

A difference and an uncertainty are never the same event

unreadable  A scanned page came back from OCR under the floor. Retina stops, says why, and asks for a readable copy.

missing_value  A field left blank by the customer. A blank is not a mismatch, so the pair is not judged on it.

What a person can do

Confirm. Correct a value. Reclassify. Upload a missing or readable file. Retry a failed job. Add a note.

A correction sits beside the model's reading, never over it, and the pair is judged again from both sides.

Every action is an append-only, labelled record of what a person decided and why.

The product · 5 of 7

Companies, ports and shipments get their own pages

A company: what our mail shows, kept apart from general knowledge marked unverified. Roles, lanes, the people on its mail.

A port: its 18 lanes on a map, placed by the world's port list, and the companies that ship through it.

Two spellings of one port are one port, joined only by a judge's verdict or a shared UN/LOCODE, never by string matching. A person can rename, merge or edit any of them, and the edit outlives every rebuild.

Retina · 13

The product · 7 of 7

Every call on record, every run scored the way the judges do

Seven calls behind one email: step, prompt version, model, latency, tokens. Every attempt is a row, whether or not it parsed.

The organisers' formula, ported line for line and proven equal to their scorer. A train and holdout split, read last.

A gate in front of the pipeline prices every email from counts, sizes and sender history before a model sees it, and holds what a budget cannot pay for. It never reads the words.

Priority by client tier, with tonnage as the tiebreaker and aging so a small client is never starved. A tier decides no category and reaches no prompt.

Retina · 14

Why this is the right solution

Four things a script, a rule set, or a plain chatbot cannot do

It reads. It has no rules to break.

No sender lists, no keyword tables, no regex over a body, no label map, no string matching between spellings. A fresh inbox with a different seed works the same, because nothing was fitted to this one.

Every verdict walks back to the line.

A value without a quote in the document is dropped, not stored. A join between spellings needs a verdict. A profile separates what the mail shows from what a model believes. Trust on a document of title needs exactly this.

The check is the entry. The ontology is the product.

Checking documents produces typed companies, ports, lanes, vessels and shipments as a side effect. The same reading answers "who ships to Savannah" without another integration.

It is measured, priced and gated.

A prompt change is a run with a score. A call is a row with a cost. An email is priced before a model reads it. A person can see every decision and reverse it. That is what makes it deployable, not a demo.
