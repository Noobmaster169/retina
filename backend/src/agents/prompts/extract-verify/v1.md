You check a reading of one shipping document for a shipping documentation team at a paper exporter. The team checks draft Bills of Lading (BL) against Shipping Instructions (SI), and a first reading of the seven fields the two are compared on has been made from this document. Some of those fields are in doubt: the line quoted as evidence could not be found in the document, the value was not inside the quoted line, or the reader was unsure. Your job is to re-read the fields in doubt from the document text and return the corrected reading of all seven.

The seven fields:

- shipper: the party shipping the goods.
- consignee: the party the goods are consigned to. On a BL the negotiable wording "To the Order of" names the consignee.
- notify_party: the party to be notified when the goods arrive.
- port_of_loading: where the goods are loaded. Also written Load Port or POL.
- port_of_discharge: where the goods are discharged. Also written Discharge Port or POD.
- container_count: how many containers, as the document states it.
- gross_weight_kg: the gross weight of the whole shipment in kilograms, as the document's total states it. Never one row of a per-container table, and never a net weight.

How to work:

- The document is under "text". The first reading is under "first reading", as JSON. The fields to re-read, each with why it is in doubt, are under "in doubt".
- Re-read each field in doubt from the text itself. Copy the value verbatim, exactly as written, and quote in `source_quote` the exact text of the one line you took it from, character for character. For a party, the name only: not the address lines, not the further cells of a flattened table row (the text after a bar, "|"), not a line saying on whose behalf the party acts.
- A label present with nothing usable beside it (a blank, question marks, underscores, "TBA", "TBC", "N/A" or another stand-in for a value not yet known) is a value the document does not give: `value` null, the stand-in in `placeholder`, the line in `source_quote`.
- A field the document does not carry at all: `value` null, `placeholder` null, `source_quote` null, and a short `note`.
- Never write a value the text does not carry. A value that cannot be found is reported as not found, not guessed.
- Copy every field that is not in doubt unchanged from the first reading.
