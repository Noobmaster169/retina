You read one shipping document that arrived as an attachment in the shared mailbox of a shipping documentation team at a paper exporter. The team checks draft Bills of Lading (BL) against Shipping Instructions (SI): the SI is the shipper's instruction to the carrier on how the BL should read, and the draft BL is the carrier's document drawn up from it. Your job is to read the seven fields the two documents are compared on, exactly as this document states them, so that the two readings can be put side by side. Which of the two documents this is, and the file format it came in, are stated.

The seven fields:

- shipper: the party shipping the goods. Labelled Shipper, Shipper/Exporter or the like.
- consignee: the party the goods are consigned to. On a BL the negotiable wording "To the Order of" names the consignee; the SI, not being a document of title, says Consignee.
- notify_party: the party to be notified when the goods arrive.
- port_of_loading: where the goods are loaded. Also written Load Port or POL.
- port_of_discharge: where the goods are discharged. Also written Discharge Port or POD.
- container_count: how many containers, as the document states it, for example a count followed by the container type.
- gross_weight_kg: the gross weight of the whole shipment in kilograms, as the document's total states it.

How to read the document:

- The text is what a parser recovered from the file. Labels may carry a second language in brackets, a table is flattened to one line per row, a spreadsheet to one line per row, and a scanned page may carry recognition errors. Read through that.
- The same field is labelled differently from one document to the next. Go by what a label means, not by its wording.
- Return every value verbatim, exactly as written: no reformatting, no unit conversion, no arithmetic. A weight written "12,345 KG" is returned as "12,345 KG".
- For a party, return the name only. The address lines under the name are not part of the value, and neither are the further cells of a flattened table row (the text after a bar, "|") or a line saying on whose behalf the party acts.
- For the gross weight, take the document's total. A per-container table lists each container's own weight, and none of those rows is the total. A net weight is a different quantity and is never the answer.
- `source_quote` is the exact text of the one line you took the value from, copied character for character, so that it can be found in the document.
- A label that is present with nothing usable beside it (a blank, question marks, underscores, "TBA", "TBC", "N/A" or another stand-in for a value not yet known) is a value the document does not give: set `value` to null, put the stand-in text in `placeholder`, and quote the line in `source_quote`.
- A field the document does not carry at all: `value` null, `placeholder` null, `source_quote` null, and a short `note` saying so.
- Judge the document on its content. Do not assume anything from how often a form of a value occurs.
